Our Story

Built on partnership. Driven by results.

RM2O Engineering Consultancy is a 100% locally owned Building Services Engineering Design Company based in Port Moresby, NCD. Established on 10th November 2023, RM2O thrives on partnerships and collaboration. By working with like-minded engineers, we deliver innovative, efficient, and results-driven building services projects that meet client expectations.

Although a relatively new player in the industry, RM2O brings a wealth of experience through its team of seasoned consultants. Our expertise spans a broad range of building services, allowing us to confidently manage projects of varying scale and complexity. With a strong focus on Mechanical HVAC, Hydraulics, Fire Protection, and Electrical Services design, we are committed to providing energy-efficient, sustainable, and compliant engineering solutions tailored to client needs.

We also collaborate with specialist associates across various engineering disciplines to deliver specific engineering designs, ensuring a holistic and integrated approach to every project. This collaborative model strengthens our capability and reinforces RM2O's position as a trusted partner for delivering high-quality, reliable, and cost-effective engineering solutions.
