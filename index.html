About RM2O

A trusted partner for high-quality, integrated building services design

RM2O Engineering Consultancy is a 100% locally owned Building Services Engineering Design Company based in Port Moresby, NCD. We thrive on partnerships and collaboration — working with like-minded engineers to deliver innovative, efficient, and results-driven projects that meet client expectations.

With a strong focus on Mechanical HVAC, Hydraulics, Fire Protection, and Electrical Services design, we're committed to energy-efficient, sustainable, and compliant engineering solutions tailored to every client's needs.
